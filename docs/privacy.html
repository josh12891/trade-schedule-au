Trade Schedule

Privacy policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · Seller Australian Dynamics (Australia) · Bundle id com.josh12891.tradeschedule

Trade Schedule is published by Australian Dynamics (Australia). It is an on-site job schedule board for Australian tradies. It is offline-first, has no ads, and does not create accounts.

This page is the public policy for the App Store and Google Play. Plain language: we do not collect a profile of you, and we do not sell data.

What we collect

Nothing that leaves the device.

  • The schedule — job name, tasks, dates, crew notes, and any trades you add — stays on your device in local storage. It is not sent to our servers. We do not run an account database.
  • PDF downloads are created on the device from that same schedule. Sharing or saving the file is something you choose in the system share sheet. We do not receive a copy.
  • No login, analytics, advertising, crash telemetry, or in-app purchases are bundled in this app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not show ads or share data with ad networks.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not require an email address, name, or location permission to use the board.

Australian users

The app is built for tradies in Australia. If we ever held personal information covered by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), we would handle it in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Today the schedule itself does not need that information to work.

Children

Trade Schedule is a trade planning tool, not a children’s app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way (for example if a future build adds optional diagnostics), we will update this page before that release ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy: australiancomsnetwork@gmail.com.
